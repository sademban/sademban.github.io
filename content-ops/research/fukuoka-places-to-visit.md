Date checked: 2026-06-09

## Places included

1. Ohori Park and nearby Fukuoka Castle Ruins
   - Official Fukuoka tourism describes Fukuoka Castle Ruins as a national historic site. Kuroda Nagamasa started construction in 1601.
   - Source: https://gofukuoka.jp/en/spots/detail/26898

2. Kushida Shrine and Hakata Old Town
   - Official Fukuoka tourism describes Hakata as Japan's largest trade port between the twelfth and sixteenth centuries.
   - Kushida Shrine is tied to Hakata Gion Yamakasa and usually displays a decorated kazari yamakasa float.
   - Source: https://www.gofukuoka.jp/articles/detail/bc5fd2b1-e507-447c-a90f-6f0704964732

3. Dazaifu Tenmangu
   - Official page lists access by Nishitetsu train to Dazaifu Station, then about 5 minutes on foot.
   - Main Hall renovation began in May 2023 and is expected to take about three years.
   - Source: https://www.gofukuoka.jp/spots/detail/26988

4. Itoshima / Sakurai Futamigaura
   - Official Fukuoka article describes Itoshima as about a 40-minute drive west from Fukuoka City.
   - Source: https://gofukuoka.jp/en/articles/detail/a4699099-7a92-48ab-b4d9-3cd29ffe0af1

5. Uminonakamichi Seaside Park
   - Official page lists access by JR Kashii Line, ferry, or bus.
   - Park hours vary by season.
   - Source: https://www.gofukuoka.jp/spots/detail/26799

6. Nanzoin
   - Official access page lists Nanzoin at 1035 Sasaguri, Kasuya District.
   - JR access: about 20 minutes by rapid train from Hakata Station, then 3 minutes walk from Kido Nanzoin-mae Station.
   - Sasaguri Tourism Association says the reclining Buddha is 41 meters long.
   - Sources:
     - https://nanzoin.net/en/access/
     - https://sasagurikanko.com/en/type-temple/%E5%8D%97%E8%94%B5%E9%99%A2/
